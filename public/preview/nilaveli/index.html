Getting here

Easier than you think

  • By air

    Helitours flies from Ratmalana, and Cinnamon Air from Katunayake, to China Bay airport, 7 km from Trincomalee.

  • By train

    The night train runs from Colombo Fort to Trincomalee. Wake up on the coast.

  • By road

    Express buses leave Colombo Pettah two or three times a day, or travel by private air-conditioned car.
