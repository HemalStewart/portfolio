Provenance

From river gravel to your hand

I

Mined

Gem gravel is lifted from shallow pits in Ratnapura by small family crews.

II

Washed

The gravel is swirled in woven baskets until the heavy crystals settle out.

III

Cut

Our cutters study each rough stone for days before the first facet.

IV

Set

Platinum or gold, made by hand in the atelier around that one stone.

Why Ceylon

The blue the world asks for

Colour
Ceylon sapphires are prized for a bright cornflower blue that stays lively in candlelight.
Hardness
9 on the Mohs scale, second only to diamond, so it lasts for generations.
History
Sri Lanka has been trading sapphires for over two thousand years.
