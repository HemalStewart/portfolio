01 / THE BEGINNING

French roots.
Vietnamese spirit.

The baguette arrived in Vietnam more than a century ago. Local cooks would turn that familiar bread into something with a flavour all its own.

1800s

02 / MADE ITS OWN

A street-food
original.

Some accounts trace the filled sandwich to Saigon’s street stalls in the late 1950s. Its exact origin remains debated; its mix of fresh herbs, pickles and savoury fillings is unmistakable.

1950s

03 / A WORD OF ITS OWN

One small bite.
A world of recognition.

In 2011, bánh mì entered the Oxford English Dictionary. A local favourite had a place in the world’s vocabulary, with countless interpretations still to come.

2011
