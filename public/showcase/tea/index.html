The estate

At 1,868 metres the clouds roll through the tea every morning. The leaf grows slowly in the cold, and slow leaf makes a brighter, more fragrant cup.

Altitude

0m

Scroll to climb the estate. The higher the garden, the slower the leaf and the lighter the cup.

  1. 0 – 600 m · Low-grown

    Bold and malty

    Fast, warm growth gives a dark, heavy liquor. Made for milk and sugar.

  2. 600 – 1,200 m · Mid-grown

    Rounded and coppery

    Cooler nights soften the leaf: a smooth, honeyed cup with body.

  3. 1,200 m + · High-grown

    Bright and floral

    Mist and cold slow everything down. The cup turns golden and fragrant, like ours.

The pluckers

“Two leaves and a bud, nothing more.”

Every leaf in a Mistline tin is picked by hand, before the sun burns off the mist. Our pluckers choose only the two youngest leaves and the bud.

It is slow work, and it is the reason the cup tastes the way it does. We pay above the estate rate and share every auction premium.
