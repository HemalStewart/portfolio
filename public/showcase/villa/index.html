The villa

Six suites on two acres of coconut garden, a few steps from the sand.

Saffron Reef sits on a quiet bay east of Tangalle. Mornings start with the sound of the surf, evenings end with dinner on the beach, and in between there is the pool, the garden and as little as you like.

Dinner where the tide comes in

Our cook shops at the Tangalle fish market each morning. At dusk the table goes down on the sand, lanterns are lit, and the menu is whatever the boats brought in.
