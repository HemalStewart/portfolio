(01) The villa

Saffron Reef sits on a quiet bay east of Tangalle, where the jungle runs down to the sand. Six suites, one long pool, and a kitchen that cooks whatever the boats bring in at dawn.

Mornings start with king coconut on the veranda. Afternoons belong to the pool, the hammocks and the slow turn of the tide. In the evening the table is set where the waves stop.

(03) A day here

Slow days, long evenings

  1. 06:00

    First light

    The bay turns gold before the village wakes. Take a coffee down to the sand and watch the fishing boats come in.

  2. 08:30

    Breakfast on the veranda

    King coconut, egg hoppers, papaya and pol sambol, served whenever you surface. Nobody here owns an alarm clock.

  3. 16:00

    The long pool

    Thirty metres of water above the ocean, warm from the afternoon sun. Tea and short eats arrive at your lounger.

  4. 19:30

    Dinner where the waves stop

    Our cook shops at the Tangalle fish market each morning. At night the table is laid on the sand, lit by lanterns.
