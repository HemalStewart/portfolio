Our story

Lakmini International emerged from a vision to make technology accessible and impactful for businesses of all sizes.

At Lakmini International we’re passionate about creating innovative software and mobile applications that drive progress and transform ideas into reality. Based in Pannipitiya, Colombo, our team of experienced developers, designers and strategists is dedicated to delivering high-quality solutions tailored to your unique needs.

Our diverse team combines deep technical expertise with a creative mindset, ensuring that every project we undertake is both technically robust and aesthetically compelling. We believe in a client-centric approach — and in staying close to our clients long after launch day.

Founder & CEO

Deshamanya Sandun Dolamulla

Sandun founded Lakmini International in 2012 with a clear conviction: technology should be accessible and impactful for every business, not only the largest. More than a decade on, that conviction still shapes every project the studio takes on.

Our approach

We start by listening.

Understanding your needs, challenges and goals comes first. Then an iterative process keeps you involved at every stage — so the final product doesn’t just meet expectations, it exceeds them.

  1. 01

    Listen

    We learn your business, your users and what success looks like for you.

  2. 02

    Plan

    Business analysis and technology consulting define the right scope, stack and budget.

  3. 03

    Design

    Intuitive, engaging UI/UX — prototyped and shaped with your feedback.

  4. 04

    Build & iterate

    Iterative development with regular check-ins, so you’re involved at every stage.

  5. 05

    Test

    Rigorous quality assurance for stable, high-performing, bug-free software.

  6. 06

    Launch & support

    On-time delivery, then ongoing maintenance and support as you grow.
