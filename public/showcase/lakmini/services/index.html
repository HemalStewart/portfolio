FAQ

Good questions.

What kinds of projects do you take on?

Custom software — enterprise systems, web applications, desktop software and integrations — plus mobile apps for iOS and Android, and consulting from business analysis to project management.

Do you build native or cross-platform apps?

Both. We build native iOS and Android apps when you need the most from each platform, and hybrid apps with React Native or Flutter when one codebase for both is the smarter investment. We’ll recommend the right approach for your goals.

How involved will I be during the project?

Very. We start by listening to your needs, challenges and goals, then work iteratively — you see progress and give feedback at every stage, so the final product matches what you had in mind.

Do you support apps after launch?

Yes. We provide ongoing maintenance and round-the-clock support to keep your applications running smoothly as your business grows.

Where are you based?

Our studio is at No 257/1/1, Old Road, Morakatiya, Pannipitiya, Sri Lanka. We’re open Monday to Friday, 9:00 AM – 5:30 PM, and you can reach us by phone, email or WhatsApp.

How do we get started?

Send us a message through the contact page or WhatsApp with a short description of your idea. We’ll get back to you to talk through your goals, timeline and budget.
